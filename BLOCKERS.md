# BLOCKERS

Honest uncertainty log. An entry here means a decision could not be made at the
required confidence with the information available, with evidence of what was
tried. Entries are removed only by resolving them, never by deleting the doubt.

Format per entry:

```
## <date> <short title>
what was needed:
what was tried (with doc links):
current state:
founder action needed (if any):
```
